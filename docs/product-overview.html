01 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Executive summary

What has been built, who it serves and how the product fits together.

Snatchi is a two-surface operational platform for audiovisual project delivery. The web application gives AV organisations a controlled workspace for projects, people, scheduling, communication, invoices, subscriptions and platform operations. The React Native mobile application gives engineers a field-focused experience for bookings, project information, documents, chat, service rates, invoicing and location-aware job progression.

The product is owned and operated by PlasmaPro Ltd and developed by Suftnet. Its design connects office coordination with field execution: an organisation creates a project and books an engineer on the web; the engineer responds and performs the work through the mobile app; both parties share status, communication, documentation and financial records through common services.

CURRENT DELIVERY POSITION
The core product and its principal commercial controls are implemented. The web production build passes, plan limits are enforced server-side, Stripe live price identifiers are preserved, and the first admin control-plane release is available. Final production release still requires environment verification, mobile release validation, live-service smoke testing and the explicitly deferred admin MFA/session-security milestone.

The value proposition

  • One operational record from project creation through booking, field delivery and invoice review.
  • Clear role separation for platform administrators, organisation owners, managers, engineers and guests.
  • Internal-team scheduling plus an external engineer network with service rates and marketplace payments.
  • Web and mobile workflows connected by shared APIs, notifications and booking-status rules.
  • A realistic three-plan commercial model with enforceable project, member and document allowances.
  • A safer administrative control plane focused on visibility, reversible access control, payment investigation and auditability.

ADMINISTRATIVE ISOLATION
Administrator accounts are control-plane identities and are completely separate from the customer lifecycle. They are not created by public registration, do not belong to a customer organisation, do not start a trial or subscription, and do not consume a plan member allowance.

Organisation boundary

Customer records are scoped to an organisation identifier. Project, user, booking, invoice and document services use that boundary so normal users operate only within their organisation. Platform admin access is separately protected through role-aware middleware, server layouts and API checks; hiding navigation is not treated as authorisation.

  4. The owner verifies their account, signs in and sees the organisation onboarding checklist.
  5. The checklist guides profile completion, first engineer, first project, first booking and optional payout setup.

06 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Connected end-to-end workflow

How web, mobile and payment services cooperate around one AV job.

  1. Project setup — the organisation creates a project with scope, customer, site, dates, priority and supporting documents.
  2. Engineer selection — an owner or manager chooses an internal engineer or searches the external network, checks availability and reviews rates.
  3. Booking offer — Snatchi creates a schedule record and notifies the engineer through in-app and push channels.
  4. Engineer response — the engineer accepts or declines in the mobile app; the organisation receives the corresponding update.
  5. Approval and payment — eligible external work is approved by the booking organisation and progresses through Stripe payment before it is ready to start.
  6. Field execution — the engineer starts the job in the app; permission-controlled geofencing can activate for the booking site.
  7. Completion — the engineer completes the work, the status is synchronised, and tracking stops.
  8. Financial record — invoice, payment and transfer records remain linked to the engineer, project and schedule for operational review.

Active-member usage includes the organisation owner, managers and engineers. Platform administrators are excluded. Active projects and active members are calculated from organisation data; document uploads are tracked within the billing period. Per-file size is checked before storage.

How enforcement works

  • The backend resolves the plan from the organisation’s recorded plan or Stripe price identifier.
  • Protected lifecycle operations require a recognised plan and an active subscription state.
  • Project, member and upload creation checks compare current usage to the plan limit before committing the operation.
  • Document size is validated independently of the period upload count.
  • Usage and entitlement information can be returned for transparent UI messaging and support investigation.
  • Live Stripe price IDs remain the existing actual Stripe identifiers; test and production IDs are selected by environment.

09 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Platform administration and operational safety

A premium first release focused on reversible, auditable controls.

  • Global paginated visibility of organisations and users.
  • Organisation detail and reversible suspension/restoration without cancelling the Stripe subscription.
  • Payment-failure register and investigation views with Stripe and reconciliation references.
  • Manual transfer retry guarded by current payment checks, destination capability checks, deterministic idempotency and a concurrency lock.
  • Mandatory operator reasons for organisation access changes and transfer retries.
  • Persistent admin audit records including actor, action, target, reason, result, time, IP address and user agent.
  • Read-only integration health reporting that indicates configuration state without returning secret values.
  • Customer-user access is read-only for platform admins in this release; permanent deletion and unaudited profile edits are intentionally excluded.

SECURITY MILESTONE NOT REPRESENTED AS COMPLETE
Admin MFA and advanced session management remain a separate release milestone. This limitation is explicitly documented and should be closed before broad production administration or high-risk operational scale.

Administrator provisioning

Administrators are provisioned only by a dedicated script with explicit test/live targets, dry-run default, hidden password input, password-strength limits and an additional confirmation for live writes. The script creates or updates only the specified identity and removes any legacy organisation association.

Recommended path to launch

A controlled sequence for converting the implemented product into a dependable live service.

  1. Freeze the release candidate — confirm web and mobile commit/version, environments and release owners.
  2. Rotate and validate production credentials — MongoDB rotation is already planned; verify all other secrets and least-privilege access.
  3. Complete the acceptance guide — run public web, owner/manager, engineer mobile, cross-role booking, all plan boundaries and authorised admin scenarios.
  4. Validate Stripe in both modes — subscriptions, customer portal, Connect onboarding, external booking payment, webhook idempotency and payout recovery.
  5. Certify mobile release builds — physical iOS/Android devices, permissions, push notifications, geofencing, deep links, crash reporting and store declarations.
  6. Complete admin security milestone — MFA, session invalidation/expiry controls and operator recovery procedures.
  7. Perform production-safe smoke tests — test records only, audit every privileged action and verify monitoring/alerts.
  8. Launch with support ownership — named incident lead, customer support route, backup/restore confidence, payment reconciliation routine and dependency-review cadence.

Go-live decision criteria

  • No open blocker or critical defect in account access, project creation, booking lifecycle, payment or data isolation.
  • All three plans display and enforce the same limits in test and production configuration.
  • Mobile release builds complete the coordinated booking workflow on supported devices.
  • Production administrators use isolated identities and the agreed MFA/session controls.
  • Stripe webhooks, email, push notifications, document storage and monitoring are verified with production-safe test records.
  • PlasmaPro has documented ownership for operations, billing support, security incidents and customer communication.

12 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Conclusion

The platform foundation is coherent, commercially understandable and ready for disciplined final release work.

Snatchi now represents more than a collection of project screens. It is a connected operating model for AV service delivery: office teams organise work on the web, engineers execute it in the field through mobile, and shared booking, payment, notification and audit services keep the lifecycle coherent.

The strongest launch story for PlasmaPro is practical: Snatchi reduces fragmentation between project planning, engineer coordination and job completion while providing a clear subscription model and a safer operational control plane. The remaining work is principally release assurance and security completion, not reinvention of the core product.

RECOMMENDED PRESENTATION MESSAGE
Snatchi gives AV organisations one connected workspace for projects, people and bookings, while giving engineers a purpose-built mobile tool for field delivery. PlasmaPro owns a credible platform foundation that can launch with a focused core and expand incrementally from real customer usage.
