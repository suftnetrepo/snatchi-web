Chapter 1

Welcome to Snatchi

Snatchi helps audiovisual (AV) companies run their jobs in one place: plan projects, book engineers, talk to your team, and keep billing in order.

There are two parts to Snatchi, and each person uses the one that suits their role.

Who you areWhat you useWhat you do
Organisation ownerWeb appSet up the company, manage billing, add people, run projects and bookings, review invoices
ManagerWeb appRun projects, schedule engineers, chat and check invoices for the same organisation
EngineerMobile appReceive bookings, accept or decline, start and finish jobs, message the office, send invoices
GuestWeb appView a simple dashboard and join project conversations

How a job flows through Snatchi

Create project→Book engineer→Engineer accepts→Chat & prepare→Work is done→Invoice & payment

The rest of this guide follows that flow. If you are brand new, start with the Quick start.

Chapter 2

Quick start: your first 30 minutes

Six steps take a new organisation from sign-in to its first booked job.

  1. Sign in with your email and the temporary password you were sent, then choose a new password (Signing in).
  2. Complete your company profile: name, description, logo and mobile number (Company profile).
  3. Add your engineers under Users (Your team).
  4. Create your first project under Projects (Projects).
  5. Book an engineer on that project (Booking an engineer).
  6. Open the conversation with the engineer to agree the details (Chat).
TipThe Dashboard has a getting-started checklist that follows these same steps and ticks them off for you. Use Dashboard tour for a guided walkthrough of the screen.
Chapter 3

Signing in and your account

How to get in, keep your account safe and get back in if you forget your password.

Sign in for the first time

  1. Go to the Snatchi website and select Sign In.
  2. Enter your Email and the temporary password from your welcome email. Use the eye icon to show what you typed.
  3. Select Sign In. You arrive on your Dashboard.
  4. Go to Settings → Password and choose a new password you do not use anywhere else.
TipIf you did not receive the welcome email, check spam first. Then use Forgot Password? on the sign-in page.

Reset a forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page select Forgot Password?.
  2. Enter your account email and submit.
  3. Open the email we send and follow the link.
  4. Choose a new password and sign in.
Good to knowReset links work once. If one stops working, request a new one.

Sign out

Open your name in the top-right of the header and choose Logout. Always sign out on shared computers.

Chapter 5

Setting up your company profile

Keep your details accurate. They appear on invoices and are what other organisations see when they work with you.

Update company details

  1. Select Settings in the left menu, then Profile.
  2. Fill in Company name, Company description, Mobile number and your Account email.
  3. Upload your Company logo.
  4. Save your changes.
TipUse a clear, square-ish logo. You can replace it at any time.

Change your password

  1. Go to Settings → Account security.
  2. Enter your Current password, then your New password and Confirm new password.
  3. Save.
Chapter 6

Building your team

People are added under Users. Each person gets a role that decides what they can do.

Understand the roles

RoleUsesTypical person
EngineerMobile appField engineer who receives and carries out bookings
ManagerWeb appOffice colleague who helps run projects and scheduling
GuestWeb appSomeone who only needs a dashboard and chat, like a client contact

Add a person

  1. Open Users and select Add user.
  2. Choose the role.
  3. Enter their name, a unique email address and contact details.
  4. For engineers, set visibility. Visible engineers can also be found by other organisations who need extra capacity.
  5. Turn on Chat access if they need to join project conversations.
  6. Save. The person receives an email with how to sign in.
CarefulEvery person needs their own email address. The same email cannot be used twice.

Find, edit and review people

  • Use Search to find someone by name or email.
  • Select Edit User to change their details, visibility or chat access.
  • Select View user documents to see files an engineer has uploaded, mostly from the mobile app.

Disable or delete someone

Use Active Status to switch a person off when they leave or pause. This keeps their past projects and bookings intact. Delete User removes the person, so prefer disabling if you may need their history.

Good to knowYour plan limits how many active people you can have (see Plans and limits). Disabling someone frees up space.
Chapter 7

Projects

A project is a job: the customer, the work, where it happens and when. Everything else, such as bookings and documents, hangs off a project.

Start a new project

  1. Open Projects and select Create project.
  2. Enter a project name and project number that your team will recognise.
  3. Describe the scope of work.
  4. Add the address where the work will happen.
  5. Choose a priority (Low, Medium or High) and a status.
  6. Set the start and end dates. The end date cannot be before the start date.
  7. Save. The project now appears in your list.
Good to knowA project must exist before you can book an engineer for the job.

Find the project you need

  • Search projects by name or number.
  • Use the status and priority filters to narrow the list.
  • Pick a date range, and select Clear to remove it.
  • The summary tiles (Total projects, In progress, Completed) give you the overall picture.

Edit a project and keep it current

  1. Select Edit Project on the project.
  2. Change the scope, dates, priority or status.
  3. Save.
TipUpdate the status as work progresses (for example Pending, then In progress, then Completed). Your tiles and reports rely on it.

Add documents to a project

  1. Select Add Documents to project.
  2. Upload files such as site photos, drawings or method statements.
  3. Open a file to check it, or remove one that was added by mistake.
Good to knowEach plan has a maximum file size and a document allowance per billing period (see Plans and limits).

Add your team to a project

Select Add Team to project to choose which of your people are working on the job.

Delete a project

Select Delete Project only when you no longer need its bookings and billing records. If the job is simply finished, set its status to Completed instead.

Chapter 8

Booking an engineer

Find the right engineer, pick a time and send a clear offer.

Book an engineer for one day

  1. Open Projects and select Book engineer on the job.
  2. Start on My engineers. Use External engineers when you need extra people from other organisations.
  3. Search by engineer name or location. Each result shows services and rate information.
  4. Select View availability and choose an available date and time.
  5. Check the title, rate, offer and job description so the engineer knows exactly what is expected.
  6. Create the booking. The engineer is notified.
Good to knowSnatchi will not let you double-book the same engineer for overlapping times.

Book several days in a row

  1. Open the engineer’s availability from the project booking flow.
  2. Choose the first and last date.
  3. Set the daily start and end time and review the working days it lists.
  4. Submit once. Snatchi creates a separate booking for every working day.
TipBecause each day is its own booking, you can change or cancel a single day without touching the others.
Chapter 9

Managing bookings

Once a booking exists it moves through a set of statuses. This tells you who needs to act next.

What each status means

StatusWhat it meansWho acts next
PendingThe offer has been sentThe engineer: accept or decline
AcceptedThe engineer agreedYou: complete any remaining step
Awaiting PaymentAn external engineer has accepted and payment is neededYou: select Pay for Service
Ready to StartAll approvals and payment are in placeThe engineer: start the job
In ProgressThe work is under wayThe engineer: complete the job
CompletedThe work is doneYou: review the invoice
Declined / CancelledThe booking will not go ahead. It stays visible for your recordsNobody. Create a new booking if needed

Work from the booking list

  • Open the booking list to see every booking and its status.
  • Select Open Conversation to message the engineer about that booking.
  • Select Pay for Service on an Awaiting Payment booking to pay securely through Stripe.
  • Select Delete Schedule to cancel a booking you no longer need. You will be asked to confirm.
Good to knowPaying for an engineer from another organisation happens inside Snatchi, in pounds, through Stripe. You never need to share card details by email or chat.
Chapter 10

Chat

Keep project conversations in one place instead of scattered across texts and emails.

Start a one-to-one chat

  1. Open Chats and make sure the Direct tab is selected.
  2. Select New chat.
  3. Choose a team member who has chat access and send your message.

Create a group

  1. Open the Groups tab and select New group.
  2. Give the group a clear name, for example the project name.
  3. Add the people who should be in it, then send your first message.

Find a conversation

Use Search conversations to look up a person or group by name. Booking conversations are created for eligible engineer bookings and can also be opened from the booking list with Open Conversation.

TipMessages go to the engineer’s phone as well. Keep them clear and polite, and put key details (access, parking, times) in the booking or project itself.
Good to knowOnly people who have chat access switched on can join conversations. If someone cannot see chat, check their record under Users.
Chapter 11

Invoices and payments

See what you are owed, what you have paid and download records for your accountant.

Find and read an invoice

  1. Open Invoices to see the Invoice register.
  2. Use Search invoices and the date range to narrow it down.
  3. Select an invoice to see its engineer, dates, line items, subtotal, tax and total.
TipThe figures at the top (such as paid and outstanding on this page) update as you filter, which is handy for a quick month-end check.

Mark an invoice paid

When an invoice has been settled, change its Status (for example from Unpaid to Paid). A cancelled invoice can be set to Cancelled.

Good to knowChanging an invoice’s status does not change the booking’s status. They are tracked separately.

Download and export

  • Open an invoice and download it as PDF (to send or print) or CSV (for spreadsheets).
  • Export the whole filtered register as a CSV for reporting or your accounts package.

Payments made and received

Snatchi also keeps a list of payments made (what your organisation paid for external engineers) and payments received (what you were paid when your engineers worked for others). Each payment shows the booking, amount and date.

Chapter 12

Your subscription and getting paid

Manage your plan, and set up payouts if other organisations will book your engineers.

Check or change your plan

  1. Go to Settings → Subscription.
  2. See your Current plan and its billing period.
  3. Select Manage your subscription to open the secure Stripe billing portal, where you can update your card, see receipts or change plan.

Set up payouts to receive money

Only needed if other organisations will book your engineers. If you only use your own engineers you can skip this.

  1. Go to Settings → Receive payments.
  2. Select Set up Stripe, or Resume setup if you stopped part-way.
  3. Complete all of Stripe’s verification questions and add your bank account.
  4. Wait until both Charges and Payouts show as enabled.
Good to knowStripe, not Snatchi, collects your identity and bank details. Have your business details and bank information to hand before you start.

Chapter 13

For engineers: using the mobile app

Everything an engineer needs is in the Snatchi app on your phone.

Get started

  1. Install the Snatchi app and tap Sign in. Use the email and temporary password from your invitation.
  2. Allow notifications so you hear about new bookings and messages straight away. Allow location if your jobs use on-site check-ins.
  3. Open Settings to complete your profile, then tap Save profile.
  4. Add your address and tap Save address.
Good to knowThe app has five tabs along the bottom: Home, Bookings, messaging, invoice and Settings.

Set your service rates

  1. In Settings, open your service rates.
  2. Tap Add first rate (or Add service rate), enter the service and your rate.
  3. Tap Save rate.
TipOrganisations see your rates when they search for engineers, so keep them up to date.

Respond to a booking

  1. Open the Bookings tab. New bookings show the project, place, dates, times, description and offer.
  2. Tap Accept booking to take the job, or Decline if you cannot.
  3. Use the booking’s conversation if you need to ask a question first.
TipReply quickly. The office is waiting on your answer to plan the job.

Do the job

  1. When it is time to begin, tap Start job. You may be asked to allow location.
  2. Add photos or documents to the project as you work.
  3. When the work is finished, tap Complete job.
Good to knowYou cannot start a job well before its booked time. If you cannot start, message the office from the booking conversation.

Messages

Use the messaging tab to read and reply to the office. Turn notifications on so you do not miss anything.

Invoices and quotes

  1. Open the invoice tab (shown as Invoices & quotes).
  2. Start a new invoice for the completed job.
  3. Tap Add line item for extras such as materials, then Save draft while you finish.
  4. Submit it. Your office can see it under Invoices on the web.

Your documents

Upload certificates and other documents from your profile. Your office can view them from your user record on the web.

Chapter 14

For guests

A guest sees a deliberately simple version of Snatchi.

  • Your menu has two items: Dashboard and Chat.
  • Use the Dashboard to see the information shared with you.
  • Use Chat to read and join the project conversations you have been added to.
Good to knowIf you need to see more, ask the organisation that invited you. They control what you can access.
Chapter 15

Getting the most out of Snatchi

Habits that keep things smooth.

  • Name things clearly. Use a consistent project number and name so anyone can find a job.
  • Keep statuses current. Dashboards and filters are only as good as the status on each project and booking.
  • Be specific in offers. Put the rate, times, access details and scope in the booking so there are no surprises.
  • Chat in Snatchi. It keeps a record and reaches engineers on their phone.
  • Disable, do not delete. Switching someone off keeps their history.
  • Check invoices weekly. A quick look at the register with a date range keeps payments from slipping.
  • Use Help & Guides. Every main screen has contextual help in the corner.
Chapter 16

Troubleshooting and FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions.

I cannot sign in.

Check your email and password, then use Forgot Password?. If it still fails, your account may be disabled. Ask your organisation owner.

I did not get the welcome or reset email.

Check your spam folder and that the email address is spelled correctly. Reset links work once, so request a new one if needed.

I cannot create a project, add a person or upload a file.

You may have reached a limit on your plan, or the file is larger than your plan allows. See Plans and limits.

The engineer I want is not in the list.

Check you are on the right tab (My engineers or External engineers), spelling of the search, and that the engineer is active. External engineers only appear if they chose to be visible.

It will not let me make a booking.

The engineer may already have a booking at that time. Try another slot.

A booking is stuck on Awaiting Payment.

An external booking needs payment before the work can start. Select Pay for Service on the booking.

Someone cannot see chat.

Their chat access must be switched on under Users.

Why is my invoice status different from my booking status?

They are tracked separately. The invoice status is about billing, and the booking status is about the work.

How do I stop someone using Snatchi?

Go to Users and switch off Active Status.

Where can I get more help?

Open Help & Guides in the left menu, or use the Contact page on the website.
