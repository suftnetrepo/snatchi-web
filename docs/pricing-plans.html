COMMERCIAL PRICING MEMO

Recommended Subscription Pricing
for Snatchi

A launch pricing structure designed to cover external operating costs and create room for sustainable growth

PREPARED FOR PlasmaPro Ltd

PRODUCT Snatchi

DATE 22 August 2026

PRICING BASIS External-service cost plan, current plan allowances and Stripe UK fee assumptions

PRICE PRESENTATION Recommended prices exclude VAT

RECOMMENDATION
Launch Basic at £79 monthly, Premium at £799 every six months, and Premium Plus at £2,990 yearly. This corrects the current inverse pricing structure, creates visible value separation and allows a small customer base to cover the recommended £190 monthly external-service budget.

WHY CHANGE IS NECESSARY
The current Premium (£250 per six months) and Premium Plus (£500 per year) both cost about £41.67 per month—less than the £50 monthly Basic plan—despite offering substantially more projects, members, uploads and file capacity. That structure weakens upgrade logic and leaves insufficient room for support and growth.

02 / PRICING RECOMMENDATION

Recommended plan structure

The customer should understand the difference without needing a sales explanation.

Basic — £79 per month

  • Up to 25 active projects.
  • Up to 10 active organisation members, including the owner, managers and engineers.
  • Up to 100 document uploads per billing period and 10MB per file.
  • All core project, booking, communication and invoice workflows.
  • Standard email support during business hours.
  • Positioning: a complete operational starting point, not a restricted trial product.

Premium — £799 every six months

  • Up to 75 active projects.
  • Up to 30 active organisation members.
  • Up to 500 document uploads per billing period and 25MB per file.
  • All core Snatchi functionality.
  • Priority email support.
  • Positioning: the recommended plan for established AV integrators running several concurrent teams.

Premium Plus — £2,990 per year

  • Up to 250 active projects.
  • Up to 100 active organisation members.
  • Up to 2,000 document uploads per billing period and 50MB per file.
  • All core Snatchi functionality.
  • Priority support and the natural home for future advanced capabilities.
  • Positioning: a high-capacity operational platform for larger organisations, not a discounted version of Premium.

The commercial difference is based on measurable capacity rather than speculative features. This keeps launch promises enforceable while allowing PlasmaPro to add higher-value capabilities incrementally.

05 / PRICING RECOMMENDATION

Pricing governance

Rules for protecting margin after launch.

  1. Publish all prices excluding VAT where appropriate and state the billing term prominently.
  2. Keep Stripe transaction fees and external-engineer marketplace fees separate in management reporting.
  3. Review acquisition, activation, retention, support time and infrastructure cost at 30, 60 and 90 days.
  4. Measure gross margin by plan; do not average a costly large customer into the whole customer base.
  5. Use entitlement usage to identify when limits are too generous, too restrictive or poorly understood.
  6. Review pricing annually and provide fair notice before changing renewal prices.
  7. Reserve custom enterprise pricing for requirements that materially change support, security, storage or integration cost.

Target economics

  • External services should remain a small proportion of subscription revenue once the customer base is established.
  • PlasmaPro should model labour and support before adopting a final gross-margin target; a 75–80% software gross-margin objective is a useful long-term management target, not a guarantee from this report.
  • Payment failures, refunds, disputes and bad debt require a separate reserve.
  • Annual and six-month plans improve cash collection but create a service obligation for the full prepaid period.
  • Premium Plus should not be discounted merely because it bills annually; its capacity can create materially higher storage, support and operational exposure.

06 / PRICING RECOMMENDATION

Approval recommendation

A clear decision for PlasmaPro.

APPROVE FOR LAUNCH
Basic: £79 monthly. Premium: £799 every six months. Premium Plus: £2,990 yearly. Prices exclude VAT. Permit the documented founding-customer offer for one initial term only, and review pricing after 90 days of real customer behaviour.

This structure gives Snatchi a credible B2B SaaS price ladder while preserving a relatively accessible entry plan. It aligns price with project, member and document capacity, supports the recommended external-service budget, and leaves room for PlasmaPro to fund the human work that is outside the operating-cost report.

The recommendation is a commercial planning model, not a guarantee of profitability. Final approval should combine this report with PlasmaPro’s labour cost, expected customer acquisition cost, tax treatment, support commitment and revenue forecast.
